About me

I turn complex work into clear, repeatable systems.

I’m Charlotte McLean, an operations professional with 6+ years of experience across real estate operations and executive support. I work at the intersection of people, processes, and tools—helping teams build structure, strengthen execution, and make day-to-day operations easier to manage.

My approach

Operations should make good work easier to repeat.

My experience spans transaction coordination, team leadership, operational training, and business operations coaching. Across those roles, I’ve worked on the practical details that keep a business moving: clear ownership, reliable handoffs, useful documentation, deadline management, performance visibility, and continuous improvement.

As a Director of Operations, I led administrative teams, refined workflows and procedures, monitored KPIs, and coordinated transaction operations. In my training and coaching work, I design learning and support structures that help remote professionals understand client systems, strengthen performance, and apply repeatable ways of working.

I’m especially interested in building systems people will actually use—not documentation for its own sake. That means understanding the real workflow, identifying where work gets stuck, making responsibilities visible, and creating tools that support consistent execution.
