Project snapshot

From individual coaching styles to a shared operating framework

The initiative addressed inconsistency in how 1:1 coaching conversations were conducted and how challenges were translated into practical follow-through. The solution combined a structured conversation flow with adaptive prompts and an action-planning discipline.

The operating challenge

Coaching quality depended too heavily on individual approach

Without a shared diagnostic structure, conversations could vary in depth, problem identification, and action clarity. The opportunity was to create consistency without making conversations feel scripted.

The design principle

Standardize the process; personalize the conversation

A common sequence creates a dependable baseline, while branching questions let coaches respond to the specific friction raised by each person.

Recruiter-facing case study

Building a scalable 1:1 coaching operating system

A process-improvement initiative focused on consistency, better diagnosis, and actionable follow-through.

01 — Business context

1:1 coaching is an important mechanism for identifying performance barriers, supporting development, and aligning expectations. The opportunity identified was to make the coaching experience more consistent while preserving the flexibility needed for individual situations.

02 — Problem definition

Coaching conversations could vary in structure and depth. Coaches needed a practical way to move beyond surface-level updates, identify underlying friction, and agree on meaningful next steps.

03 — Solution design

I developed an interactive coaching playbook organized around a value-first conversation sequence. Version 6.0 opens on the Call Flow overview and provides distinct navigation for the Friction Tree, Objections Wizard, Scenarios, and Reframing. It includes expanded opening scripts, a five-category review of wins and progress, deeper-probing pathways, and an action-planning framework with ownership, measurables, obstacles, support, and follow-up checkpoints.

04 — Technology enablement

The methodology was translated into an interactive web-based resource. The interface supports navigation across coaching stages, searchable prompts, scenario exploration, and decision-based guidance. The portfolio describes the delivered functionality without making an unverified claim about the development tools used.

05 — Implementation & adoption

The framework was introduced for use by coaches beyond the original creator. This extended the approach from an individual resource into a shared coaching method.

06 — Results

Following implementation, coaches became more consistent in conducting 1:1 sessions, coaching feedback scores improved, and other coaches adopted the framework. Quantitative figures should be added only when supported by records.

07 — Operational capabilities demonstrated

  • Identifying process gaps and defining an operational problem.
  • Designing a standardized yet adaptive workflow.
  • Translating a methodology into a usable digital tool.
  • Supporting implementation and cross-team adoption.
  • Connecting process design to feedback and continuous improvement.
