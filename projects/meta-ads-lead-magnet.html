Process case study

A source-grounded interpretation of the six-page Facebook & Instagram Ads Playbook (TBJT January 2026).

Workflow design

  • Prepares a property landing page and lead capture page in KW Command Site.
  • Uses a PDF copy of the MLS as input to Ads Optimizer GPT for ad creative details.
  • Sets up an Auction campaign in Ads Manager, then configures budget, destination, performance goal, audience, placements, extensions, and creative.
  • Publishes the ad, waits for approval, and reviews performance at Day 1, Day 3, and Day 7.

Operational checkpoints

  • Confirm the correct property-specific lead capture landing page is attached.
  • Keep the ad focused on one landing page; Site Links are switched off in the source workflow.
  • Use one image or one video, and confirm the form is visible and working.
  • Use the GPT Meta Ads Performance Review by pasting a screenshot of performance numbers and adjusting as advised.
  • Retain a future migration path to a Sales objective and Lead event when the Pixel is ready.

Source-specific configuration reference

Campaign level

Buying type: Auction. Objective: Traffic or Engagement in the setup section. Budget strategy: Ad set budget. Special Ad Category: Housing.

Ad set level

Website destination for traffic and leads; maximize landing page views; leave cost per result blank; $1/day and 10-day schedule in Step 4.

Audience & delivery

Step 5: four cities, 50-mile radius, remove statewide targeting; age 18–65+, all genders; detailed targeting blank or minimal. Advantage+ Placements.

Creative & review

One image or video; copy primary text, headline, and description from Ads Optimizer prompt; CTA Learn More or Get More Info. Review Day 1, 3, and 7.

Scope: This is a portfolio demonstration of the supplied instructions, not a live Meta integration. It does not publish ads, validate compliance, connect to Ads Manager, access campaign analytics, or claim campaign performance results.

Audience-specific drip campaign flows

These schedules are transcribed from pages 3–4 of the Lead Magnet Playbook. Each flow exits when a reply is received and endorses the lead to an agent.

Buyers

Goal: Educate buyers, reduce uncertainty, and identify timeline.

  1. Day 0: Deliver buyer guide.
  2. Day 2: Soft check-in; if reply, exit drip and endorse to agent.
  3. Day 6: Education on costs, financing, or timing.
  4. Day 12: Clarify common buyer misunderstandings.
  5. Day 18: Low-pressure invitation to ask questions or request a plan.
  6. Day 25: Move to Buyer Lead Pipeline.
Sellers

Goal: Build pricing confidence and identify seller motivation.

  1. Day 0: Deliver seller guide.
  2. Day 3: Check-in to identify seller stage; if reply, exit and endorse.
  3. Day 7: Market insight on why some homes do not sell.
  4. Day 14: Mistake avoidance around pricing and preparation.
  5. Day 21: Offer a price range or consultation.
  6. Day 28: Move to Seller Lead Pipeline.
Senior Relocation (SRS)

Goal: Reduce overwhelm and build trust through education and reassurance.

  1. Day 0: Deliver senior planning guide.
  2. Day 3: Gentle check-in; if reply, exit and endorse.
  3. Day 8: Timing and family coordination.
  4. Day 15: Emotional and logistical concerns.
  5. Day 22: No-pressure support conversation invitation.
  6. Day 30: Move to Senior Lead Pipeline.
New Construction

Goal: Provide cost clarity and guide build decisions.

  1. Day 0: Deliver new construction guide.
  2. Day 3: Check-in on common build surprises; if reply, exit and endorse.
  3. Day 7: Cost clarity on upgrades and site costs.
  4. Day 14: Build-versus-buy comparison.
  5. Day 21: Offer a walkthrough of options.
  6. Day 30: Move to New Construction Lead Pipeline.
Source fidelity: The lead magnet document specifies a two-week rotation, an eight-week campaign review, and four separate drip flows. It does not provide measured lead conversion, response, or pipeline results; none are claimed here.
