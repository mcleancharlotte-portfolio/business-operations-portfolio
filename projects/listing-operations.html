Process case study

A practical operations artifact built around the source playbook’s actual sequence, role assignments, timing cues, document checks, and exception handling.

Workflow architecture

The process is organized around the lifecycle of a listing and its transaction. Each stage pairs an agenda of operational actions with status, notes, key dates, and supporting checklists.

  • Pre-listing setup and listing readiness
  • Live-date actions and recurring weekly marketing-table reviews
  • Accepted-offer intake, stakeholder introductions, and transaction setup
  • Inspection and contingency follow-through
  • Closing preparation, settlement, status updates, and post-close outreach

Operational controls embedded in the source

  • Role ownership is indicated by LM, TC, or Agent.
  • Timeline cues include Live Date, Week 1–9, after contingency removal, and countdowns to closing.
  • Document checklists distinguish required items from conditional items.
  • Notes call out special handling, such as not sending a Personal Property Agreement to the lender unless the agent directs otherwise.
  • Transaction dates, included/excluded items, and an addenda/amendment tracker support coordination.
